## Bounded outcome

If no eligible packet survives acquisition/reference gates, finish this as an acquisition/eligibility result with an offline frozen ledger and independent hash/denominator audit. Do not run a nominal zero-case accuracy experiment. If eligible packets exist, run them once in a new immutable directory after tests; record durable status, code/input/output hashes and partial failures, independently verify all saved measures and experiments 1–11. Do not edit execution-frozen code after running. Fresh natural data becomes development after its outcomes are inspected.

This pilot can test transfer of the fixed retrieval/alignment procedure to newly acquired source components and expose contradictions in proposed mappings. It cannot establish broad decipherment, general rejection reliability or semantic meanings from positional agreement. Additional independent human review and additional source components are prerequisites for semantic promotion.
