# Experiment 58: two-slot closer numeric-comparator feasibility intake

Question: can two fixed searches and at most two new page captures supply a
development comparator that passes the existing native intake rule AND shares
at least THREE distinct numeric key types with the frozen masked target, after
the unchanged structural/quotation masks? E56's existing four comparators share
at most one type. No LCS/window/control scores, comparisons, lexical predictions,
unit normalization or meanings are evaluated in this feasibility intake.

Before either search, freeze this protocol/config/code/tests, prior URL snapshot,
E56 target inventory, existing results and all 57 prior manifests/seals. The
target numeric inventory is unchanged: use ALL retained numeric key types, not
only the six reference-shared types audited in E57. Literal matching and the
three-type threshold cannot change after acquisition.

Exactly two sequential web.run calls, each one fixed search_query with long
response, as in config. Queries intentionally include already exposed numeric
strings; this is development selection, not blind validation. No retries,
query repair, extra filters, opens, clicks, archives or replacement searches.
Search date/number operators are planning hints; native data decide eligibility.
Save exact intents/UTC times before and exact responses/errors after each call.
Supported result formats are E55 strings/MCP text blocks; otherwise slots remain
empty. Use only attributable Title (https://URL) / wordlim blocks in returned
order, preserving spans/hashes and every rejection.

Snapshot prior registry/results and linked/declared URLs in saved publisher HTML,
adding all E55 results and two E55 native captures to the earlier snapshot.
Preserve input hashes and all prior tags. Explicit E58 policy: previously linked
or search-only URLs MAY be nominated as labeled exposed development leads;
EVERY previously captured article URL is excluded, including failed v50 slots,
the known reference and v55 pages. This differs from E55's broader exclusion of
all prior exposures, without rewriting that decision. No familiarity/independence
or untouched status is inferred from an absent tag. No recapture of failed slots.

Choose the first eligible result per query, at most ONE each. Eligibility for
nomination is unchanged E55 strict HTTPS physicsworld.com /a/ ASCII slug safety
and whole-word topic condition (neutrino/neutrinos plus ANITA/Antarctic/Antarctica)
on title/result block. Across queries, all valid first appearances enter a global
seen set, including excluded or over-cap hits. Native date, title, transport,
scope or numeric failure never triggers another nominee. Freeze two nominees/
empty slots and capture preflight before any page request.

At most TWO initial native routes, one per occupied slot, using the sealed E53
curl helper (HTTPS/TLS verified, retry zero, disabled curl config, max three
HTTPS redirects, 8-second connect/20-second total timeout, 4 MiB body limit).
No persistent credentials/cookies, scripts/forms, challenge bypass or mirrors.
Preserve raw bodies/headers/telemetry/receipts locally, including failures.

Apply all fourteen E54 native checks unchanged, including [2000-01-01, 2018-09-30]
publication dates, three metadata title channels, present-h1 consistency,
original body/URL identity, no challenge, E52 paragraph runs, minimum three runs/
1000 characters and topic screen. Expected title is frozen from search, with
only the final service suffix removed. No late-date or missing-field exception.

As in E55, reserve at most ONE exact observed alternate og:url route per slot
only when native transport, final/all canonical URLs and all three titles match,
and exactly one distinct safe noncanonical og:url exists. Dates/numeric overlap
do not influence reservation. Freeze each native artifact hash/alternate plan
before its request. Direct og:url needs none; unsafe/ambiguous/unavailable values
get none. Use identical transport limits and E53's five route checks. E54 still
requires a positive observed redirect to the expected canonical endpoint; label
any new binding saved_v58_redirect with v58 evidence provenance. No inferred URL.
At most TWO alternate routes, FOUR routes/SIXTEEN application requests total
including redirects. GitHub publication traffic is outside the research budget.

For every occupied slot, retain its native decision and, if structural scope is
available, the full E56 representation: original E52 paragraph runs; E46 balanced
curly/ASCII-double quote masks; original-boundary numeric/uppercase extraction.
Unsupported quotes make numeric representation unavailable without replacement.
Uppercase entries retain representation continuity; no uppercase score is run.
Report candidate numeric occurrence count and the DISTINCT key intersection with
the unchanged target in target first-occurrence order. Repeats cannot inflate
the threshold; 100.000 is not 100,000, and no unit/year/decimal repair is allowed.
Compute this inventory even if another native check fails, labeling the failure.

A qualifying harder comparator requires native eligibility, available quote-
masked representation and >=3 shared numeric types. Report every slot and unmet
criterion. Feasibility passes if at least ONE of TWO slots qualifies; two-of-two
fill is separate. Empty slots are failures, not absent denominators. If none
qualifies, stop at budget with the registered negative result. Do not extend
searches or lower the threshold. Old E50/E54 capacity failures, E55 capture
capacity, E56 scores, E57 context limits and earlier synthetic failures stay fixed.

All new material is exposed development data. No certified false parent, source
independence/identity, quantity equivalence, opaque meanings, independent review
