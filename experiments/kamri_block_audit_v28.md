Block owner is the nearest ancestor in p, blockquote, li, h1–h6, td/th, div, nav, section, article, otherwise the root post-body. Group only consecutive atoms with the same owner path; a repeated noncontiguous owner makes another run. Keep every join separator and final newline explicitly so text-plus-separator coverage is an exact partition. Footnote-rule candidates are horizontal edges at or below 55% of page height, at least 20% of page width long and starting at or before 35% of page width.
