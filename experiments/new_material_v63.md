# Experiment 63: one non-ANITA source-first material recovery pilot

## Justification and frozen choice

E62 closed the ANITA/publisher comparator route. Do not reopen it. Independent
review is still unavailable, and old source groups cannot become untouched.
The prior E37 source-first intake nevertheless retains a separate material gap:
post01, Quantum Semantic Fields and Universals, has only indexed extraction;
its raw body/citation inventory was unavailable. Recovering its bibliography
could provide a different work candidate before any correspondence modelling.
This is acquisition feasibility, not an assumption of source-group novelty.

Select the **first E37 nominee other than post00**, preserving original order:
post01 at the exact saved URL including `?m=0`. It was already exposed through
search/indexed material. No new discovery, translation, lexicon or source claim
motivates its rank. The known title prefix is the original title before its
subtitle colon. All original E37 failures stay unchanged. Freeze the nominee,
old known-exposure URLs plus the later known arXiv identifier, old connected-group
ledger, E62 closeout and all 62 prior manifests/seals, code and fixtures.

## One combined finite material pilot

At most three routes: (1) one Internet Archive CDX query for the exact URL;
(2) only if eligible, its exact selected raw `id_` replay; (3) only if target
provenance passes, one explicitly cited, previously unrecorded arXiv source.
Each route: HTTPS-only including redirects, zero retries, 8 s connect / 20 s
total, at most three redirects, 4 MiB for index/replay and 20 MiB for source.
Maximum twelve application responses. Use the sealed E53 acquisition helper
and the fixed v63 user-agent, with intent/limits saved before each attempt.
No browser fallback, canonical/mobile-URL alternative, second archive timestamp,
extra post, replacement citation, new search, robots/assets or extra batch.

The exact CDX URL is frozen in JSON, using the known E38 endpoint: exact match,
JSON, status200, text/html, timestamp/original/statuscode/mimetype/digest fields,
latest one before the frozen UTC cutoff. Require exactly one record, exact
original string including query, valid timestamp/cutoff and base32 SHA1 digest.
A matching URL is archive metadata, not proof of post wording at publication.
An empty/malformed/blocked response stops this pilot without replacement.

Replay must complete HTTP200 at the exact selected replay URL (including m=0),
contain exactly one nonempty post-body, include the known title prefix in the
HTML/post title, and bind all canonical/og fields to the same HTTPS host/path
with either no query or the exact observed m=0 rendering parameter. This narrow
identity rule is declared before acquisition; it does not equate arbitrary
queries. Final-response Memento timestamp must match CDX and SHA1 of saved
content-decoded raw bytes must equal its digest. Reject a digest-layer mismatch;
do not repair, strip query, relax the gate or choose another snapshot.

Export only after all gates pass: remove script/style/noscript within the unique
post-body, join its text with spaces, retain source hash/timestamp. This is dated
archival raw material, not authenticated publication-year or current wording.
Do not display or compare opaque paragraphs in the prediction process.

## Explicit source metadata and retained uncertainty

Reuse E37's citation inventory over the unique raw post body, retaining all
links and rejection reasons. Consider only explicitly linked arXiv identifiers,
in original order. Preserve DOI/other records but do not invent routes for them.
Reject identifiers already in the frozen URL inventory regardless of version;
choose the first remaining arXiv citation and retain later ones as over-cap.
Map its declared abstract/PDF route deterministically to the official PDF as
in E37; save that exact observed/derived route before the single source attempt.
Absence from the known inventory means provisional novelty only. Mirrors,
composite components and source-group independence remain unconfirmed.

Preserve the raw source and receipt. A PDF header means raw_pdf_unreviewed,
not verified prose, edition, full-source coverage or correspondence. Do not
parse/render the PDF, train, align, translate, use copied names/numbers as meaning,
or label an existing source as a fresh independent work. No reviewer is supplied,
contacted or fabricated. No review packet is represented as a review return.
A later material review must separately bind edition, coverage and source groups.

## Outcomes, checks and stop

Keep one post and one source slot even when unavailable. Distinguish index
availability, archival identity/digest gate, raw target recovery, explicit source
candidate selection, raw source material, connected-work novelty, review access
and untouched capacity. Qualifying independently reviewed untouched works stay
zero without new evidence. The primary useful outcome is a newly recovered
material dossier or a precise acquisition failure, not semantic acceptance.

Fixtures cover exact-query CDX identity, digest/Memento/URL binding, body/title
ambiguity, first-identifier order/cap/known exclusions and absent citations. A
separate offline verifier checks request timing/commands/artifacts, archive gates
and inventories, all prior runs/seals, and no comparison/admission counters.
Shared parsing/helper code is disclosed; same-agent checks are not scientific
review. Test the local and isolated public suites, seal artifacts, checkpoint raw
material locally and publish only code/protocol/small reports. Stop after this
single post/source pilot. A failure does not authorize another post or archive
query in the same experiment. E62's route closure remains intact.
