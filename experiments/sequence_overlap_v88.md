# E88: exact repeated-sequence audit of saved development gains

## Question and boundary

Do E79's saved gains coincide with exact multi-token sequences shared between
its evaluation group and the other twelve training groups? This is a read-only
attribution diagnostic on exposed development material. E79's model comparison
is stopped; no model is fitted, no probability predicted, and no gate retested.
New-material confirmation and the independently reviewed semantic benchmark stay
unlaunched. E85/E87 acquisition failures and all earlier results are preserved.

The justification is narrower than confirmation: E76 removed complete duplicate
units but did not establish absence of shorter cross-group repetitions. E79's
shuffle comparison destroys such sequences. Their contribution is an unresolved
alternative worth quantifying before more acquisition or interpretation.

## Frozen scope and measurement

Use exactly E77/E79's 13 groups, 98 retained E76 units and 1,918 saved target
pairs. Use original E76 token forms (not pooled UNK categories), masks, unit
boundaries and pair flags. Never join across a barrier. Reconstruct maximal
unblocked, pair-connected segments. Each evaluation group is compared only with
its other twelve groups. Excluded units and the held-out group never enter its
training index. No source prose, other snapshots or network resources are read.

For EVERY n in the fixed range 2 through 8, enumerate every contiguous n-token
window in every allowed training and evaluation segment. Exact tuple equality
of stored forms is the only matching rule. Keep all matching occurrence pairs,
including repeated windows in one unit or several groups, with UID, group and
original token indices. No stemming, fuzzy matching, category collapsing,
threshold search or source/semantic adjudication. Length eight is a measurement
cap, not a claim that no longer repetition exists.

An evaluation TARGET PAIR is covered at length n when both its context and target
lie inside the same matched n-window. Mere inclusion of the target without its
context is insufficient. Store every pair's covered lengths and its maximum
covered length (0 if even its bigram is absent, otherwise 2..8). This max is capped
at eight. Separately record whether the saved E79 probability differs from its
unigram; do not treat all eligible contexts as changed predictions.

Read E79's original unigram/observed probabilities and compute only their saved
log2(P/U) gain decomposition. Report each group's complete count and gain sum;
for each n report covered/uncovered counts, positive and negative gain sums and
their contributions divided by the ORIGINAL group pair count. Equal-group macro
contributions use all thirteen groups, including zero contributions. Also report
exclusive capped-max bins and fraction of positive gain mass covered, using
original equal-group weighting. Empty conditional means/fractions are null,
never a reason to drop a group. The covered and uncovered contributions must sum
to E79's unchanged macro gain. Keep positive and negative masses separate to
avoid interpreting a net cancellation ratio as concentration.

This is a descriptive spectrum, with no success threshold, winner selection,
new subgroup score, significance claim, controls, rescore or revised E79 gate.
Exact shared text may be ordinary recurring language, a template, a copied
residue or generator output. Overlap neither proves leakage nor establishes
source ancestry. Absence under this rule does not exclude nonexact templates,
other contamination, grouping errors or invalid language masks.

## Execution, verification and stop

Test synthetic fixtures before freezing this protocol, new code, E76/E77/E79
seals and the latest E87 seal into one registration. Then perform exactly one
execution into a new run directory, refusing overwrite. Hash-bind inputs and
output ledger/report. Preserve a failed attempt without repair under its ID.

A separate verifier imports no production overlap/model code. It reconstructs
segments and matches by diagonal equal-run enumeration rather than window-index
lookup; reproduces all occurrence pairs, target coverage and saved-gain
partitions; checks original targets, probability bindings, all denominators,
original metrics and frozen seals. This is computational reconstruction, not an
independent scientific reviewer or authentication of source meanings.

Stop after the complete 2..8 spectrum regardless of outcome. Do not remove
matched passages, merge groups, change the model/mask, collect a third listing,
try another cutoff or call the residual contribution confirmation. Any later
intervention needs its own justified protocol. Raw token windows/coordinates and
ledgers remain local-only; public code, synthetic fixtures and aggregate report
follow PUBLICATION.md. Natural semantic qualifying works remain 0/4.
