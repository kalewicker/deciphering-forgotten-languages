# Experiment 15: public alignment-package audit

Registered 2026-10-01 before acquiring or opening the public shared package. Scope: audit the package linked by the public Kamri inventory author, preserve its proposed translations as quarantined annotations, and verify recoverable proposed passages against independent source artifacts. No translation model, induction, thresholds, lexical promotion or scored generalization experiment is authorized by this audit.

The public claim is 24 Kamri-proper posts and 978 units, 23 continuously source-aligned and one provisional. Treat those as hypotheses to check, not immutable facts or acceptance criteria. Account for every provided file, article and unit; retain missing and inconsistent records in the inventory. Separate Kamri Western, derived lexicons, annotation references, original FL copies, and original English source copies. Do not silently drop missing rows, deduplicate the denominator, overwrite translations or repair source text without recorded transformations.

Acquire the single shared ZIP through its public download link (standard dl=1 parameter), preserving raw bytes, headers, redirect URLs, UTC timestamps and SHA-256. A failed environment-related network request may receive one disclosed identical outside-sandbox diagnostic. No authentication, subscription or challenge bypass. Inspect archive member names, declared sizes and types before reading; reject traversal, symlinks, duplicate names, excessive expanded sizes and executable archive contents. Do not execute package code. Keep package lexicons quarantined and out of induction.

Audit all available annotation rows for schema, stable article/unit identities, numbering/coverage, empty or duplicated records, source identifiers, exact and normalized counts, confidence/provisional boundaries, stated totals and source component leakage. Use the frozen v14 conservative co-occurrence components; report any proposed refinement separately. Never treat author-provided English as independent source confirmation. Keep structural/positional compatibility separate from validated lexical meaning.

For independent passage verification, start with all persisted inspectable English source artifacts and exact FL snapshots already in the workspace. Record exact and whitespace-normalized occurrences, source/source-edition identity, transformations, offsets, ambiguity and coverage. A match to a prior indexed or unverified extract must remain labeled as such. Partial coverage qualifies only the concretely supported units, never the rest of an article. Source authenticity and correspondence are distinct gates. New source acquisition, if necessary, is bounded to twelve explicitly identified public author/publisher/library routes across the package; no result-dependent replacement of sources or widening of passages. PDF material used requires image review of relevant source pages. Save unavailable and excluded routes without filling gaps from the package itself.

Produce an immutable audit run and a human-readable report, including file/row denominators, a verification-status ledger for every recoverable unit and article, explicit unresolved claims and a source-component split readiness assessment. Freeze reviewed split eligibility, not fabricated complete folds when verification is insufficient. Existing components remain exposed; this audit cannot restore untouched status. Independently verify input/code/output hashes, audit accounting, lexicon quarantine, and preservation of experiments 1–14. Further review may be needed before any scientific or semantic acceptance.
