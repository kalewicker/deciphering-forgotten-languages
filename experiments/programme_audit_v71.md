# E71: offline programme closeout audit

This is a registered retrospective audit of already exposed E1–E70 evidence,
not a new hypothesis test, independent review or natural rescore. Registration
precedes the integrity sweep and claim-ledger computation; the major historical
outcomes were known when this protocol was written.

Freeze all 70 completed run manifests, all existing E15–E70 deliverable seals,
the selected JSON-pointer assertions and this audit's code in the accompanying
JSON registration. Check every input/code/output/artifact hash in those manifests
and every deliverable hash in the seals. Preserve the E1 normalized-text hashing
convention. Cache by path only within one execution. Check exact JSON types as
well as values (false is not a numeric zero).

The fixed claim ledger covers natural evidence capacity, the synthetic
source-absence failure, the ANITA numeric panel and terminal comparator failure,
and the second post's literal screen and terminal wording search. All selected
claims and all historical inventories stay in the denominator. Any missing file,
hash mismatch or assertion mismatch fails the audit; do not repair old evidence.
Emit the full check ledger locally and a small public receipt. A passing audit
means preservation/accounting only; it cannot promote a claim to semantics or
independent validation. It is not a replay of every historical algorithm.

Produce one closeout report organized by evidence family, with supported claims,
failed/unresolved claims, closed routes and one concrete evidence-admission
boundary for future work. Distinguish a proposed next step from an executed one.
Retain the four-work benchmark requirement (two development/two test, at least
two independently reviewed nonoverlapping passages each). Additional material
availability alone does not meet it. No new search, download, reviewer contact,
prediction, model change or semantic admission is authorized by this audit.

Stop after the report, integrity receipt, relevant checks and publication.
Do not turn an unavailable reviewer into an automatic second-model substitute
or start another parser/search repair on the closed E62/E70 routes.
