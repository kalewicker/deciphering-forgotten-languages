# E71: programme closeout and the next evidence boundary

The project has a preserved research pipeline, useful conditional alignment
results and documented failure modes. **It has not validated a natural FL
decipherment.** The next meaningful advance requires independently defensible
evidence about passages or meanings. More acquisition repairs on the closed
routes would add engineering work without resolving that requirement.

This is a retrospective, offline audit of exposed evidence through E70. It
introduces no new data, predictions, reviewer return or semantic acceptance.
The [registration](../experiments/programme_audit_v71.md) fixes the scope; the
[claim ledger](../experiments/programme_audit_v71_revision1.json) binds exact historical
values and denominators. These are evidence families, not 70 independent
scientific advances.

## What changed since the earlier progress review

The [earlier review](progress_review_2026-10-20.md) recommended one final
capacity-qualified ANITA comparator batch. E62 exercised that stopping rule.
E63–E70 then recovered a different exposed post, identified a cited paper,
screened it literally and exhausted a separate bounded wording route. Neither
branch produced a qualifying new benchmark work or an independent review.
Metadata/parser repairs preserved their original failed gates; they did not
retroactively create prospective successes.

These are exhausted registered designs under specified budgets. They do not
show that decipherment is impossible or that every possible source has been
searched. They do show why continuing the same procedure is presently a poor
use of effort.

## One proposed next step: resolve review access on an existing packet

Before acquiring more material or adding model features, obtain **one completed
independent source/passage review of one existing E25 packet** under a separately
registered intake. This is a proposed resource-dependent step, not a reviewer
assignment or a new experiment launched by E71. The user previously reported
having no reviewer; that resource remains unresolved. No external contact was
made here.

Use a fixed packet selected before the return, preserve its existing bytes and
window, and supply no model translations or proposed opaque glosses. The return
must identify the reviewer and human/automated role, prior exposure, relationship
to the predictions, packet/text hashes, exact source/target spans, contrary
evidence, uncertainty and the scope of any relationship claimed. Assess the
independence declaration and evidence, not merely the return's schema. Do not
count a fresh model session or automated replay as independent semantic review
by default. A rejected or uncertain relationship is a useful completed review;
no invented positive correspondence is required.

This single review would test whether a credible review route is feasible.
Even a supported correspondence would remain exposed development evidence and
would not validate opaque meanings or satisfy the natural benchmark. The
existing benchmark requirement stays **four new independent connected works**,
two development and two test, each with at least two independently reviewed,
nonoverlapping passage units and hash-bound artifacts. Current qualifying
capacity remains **0/4**. Test answers must be withheld before prediction; a
source-disclosed packet cannot be relabeled untouched.

If no suitable review route can be secured, leave natural evaluation unlaunched
and the evidence requirement unresolved. Do not manufacture another experiment
to conceal that constraint. There is no new evidence in this audit that justifies
another query variant, a broad crawl, additional alignment features, language
graphs or translation claims. E62/E70 remain closed; synthetic feature expansion
remains stopped. A materially different proposal needs its own falsifiable
question, resource justification and fixed stopping rule.
