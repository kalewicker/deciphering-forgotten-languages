# Experiment 43: two whole queries present; six remaining longer blocks absent

The frozen Physics World page body contains **2/8 complete queries**: the
known quote00 and quote01, the expanded instrument name. It contains **1/7
longer blocks**, entirely the known positive. **0/6 remaining longer blocks**
(quote02 through quote07) occurs in full. All eight outcomes are available.
The registered new primary question therefore returns **false**.

This is whole-query presence in the saved representation. It does not reject
partial reuse, editing or paraphrase, and is not a measure of opaque FL meaning.

quotation and the expanded name. None of the other six complete longer blocks
is present. This result does not establish which source first published the
quotation, historical 2018 contents, copying direction, immediate source identity
for opaque FL, full-post coverage, translation or word meanings. No independent
review or untouched natural test unit is admitted. Natural-benchmark capacity
remains unmet; scientific acceptance and semantic metrics remain null.
