# E73: the fresh review is useful, but its documented evidence coverage is incomplete

The first isolated Codex review omitted both unusual spelling clues already
recorded for Risk mathematics. Its correct offsets and valid JSON therefore
do not make it an exhaustive evidence assessment. It also proposed one distinct
opening-passage pair, which remains explicitly uncertain. Preserve both facts:
the review adds a localized proposal while leaving known observations undocumented.

This is a **retrospective documentation audit**, not another linguistic trial or
a semantic-recall estimate. It uses only the unchanged E72 return, E26 appendix
and E25 packet. The [registration](../experiments/review_coverage_v73.md) fixes all
four earlier Risk pairs, all four unmatched English blocks and both spelling
cues. The coordinator had already inspected these materials and suspected the
omissions before registration; no outcome blindness is claimed. No reviewer was
recontacted, no second packet reviewed and no natural target rescored.

All old pairs remain in the denominator: **1/4** have an enclosed FL interval,
**1/4** an enclosed source interval, and **0/4** have both intervals enclosed
within one returned evidence/counterexample pair. The separate R1 sightings must
not be joined into a relationship the reviewer did not assert. This is exact
documentation overlap, not a claim that the reviewer mentally overlooked every
uncited passage or failed a preregistered review-accuracy threshold.

Of the four previously recorded unmatched English blocks R5–R8, **1/4** is
enclosed by returned exact FL spans: R5, the legal/operational passage. The return
also discusses the other blocks' topics in notes. Their lack of exact-span
citations does not establish that they were unread or unconsidered. R5 is cited
twice in E72; that remains one distinct old block.

Both unusual spelling clues remain genuine literal observations in the original
packet: each old FL slice equals its source slice exactly. Their omission limits
the fresh review's completeness. It does **not** establish a source identity or
meaning. The earlier E26 copied-anchor rule still makes false nominations in
all three engineered source-absent frames, including the Risk spelling-pair
frame. Reintroducing the clues cannot erase that failed specificity gate or make
two spellings into independent works.

## The distinct new proposal stays distinct

E72's sole evidence pair joins FL `[645,887)` with source `[3176,3451)` in the
unchanged packet coordinate systems. It has no two-sided interval overlap with
the four E26 Risk pairs. This is a distinct localized proposal relative to that
selected appendix, not new material, work-level independence or a validated
statement correspondence. Its basis and relationship remain `structural_only`
and `uncertain`.

The E30 Risk query concerned a different paragraph, `[2893,3480)`. Its failed
repetition-pattern result cannot simply be assigned to E72's opening pair.
Likewise, E27's failed literal-context screen does not disprove a nonliteral
relationship. Those failures remain relevant limits on the proposed methods;
they do not answer a different hypothesis by substitution. No new structural
score or gloss was computed here.

## Decision

Classify E72 as a **completed automated proposal with incomplete documented
evidence coverage**, rather than a final source/passage adjudication. Keep its
first return unchanged. A future separately authorized review should have a
neutral inventory of observable clues and require explicit supported/rejected/
uncertain disposition of every item, with opposing evidence retained. Disclosure
of that inventory would itself be exposure and would prevent treating the result
as a fresh discovery test. Do not turn this recommendation into a repair loop on
the already completed E72 review.

There is no evidence here to launch a natural lexical/translation benchmark or
resume the stopped pattern-feature programme. The review route demonstrated
operational isolation, not independent scientific acceptance. A stronger source
claim still needs a new constraint beyond copied clues and local resemblance;
none was supplied by this audit. The natural benchmark remains **0/4 qualifying
works** under the unchanged requirement. E62/E70 stay closed, and synthetic
feature expansion stays stopped. This audit completes the follow-up on the
first return; it authorizes no further model review, packet expansion or search.
