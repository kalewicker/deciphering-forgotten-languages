# Experiment 31: fixed-pattern sensitivity works narrowly; source-specific admission fails

The synthetic calibration completes and independently replays all **1,000 panels**, but the registered **synthetic candidate-localization admission criterion fails**. The unchanged v30 scorer recovers every feature-available bijective parent, is brittle under edits/contextual forms, and cannot distinguish a true parent from a constructed repetition-equivalent alternative when that alternative occupies the same candidate slot.

All **255 tests pass**. The separate verifier reconstructs all 40 parents, 200 query origin traces, 440 public views, 1,200 distinct query/candidate scores, 40 equivalence certificates and 200 paired-world comparisons. All 30 prior run inventories and earlier deliverable hashes verify unchanged. No natural target is rescored, and no natural source identity or word meaning is admitted.

## Known generated truth and fixed denominators

The assay crosses ten symbolic structural profiles with four relabeling/distractor keys. Six profiles use fixed hash-generated class streams; four deliberately probe unavailable features: all-unique, one repeated class, too short and densely blocked. Five mechanisms produce 200 queries, and five panel conditions produce 1,000 cases. These are dependent factorial fixtures: the four keys reuse each profile's underlying class pattern, and mechanisms/panels reuse parents. They are not 40 independent natural texts or 1,000 independent population trials.

The five mechanisms are consistent renaming, an insertion after every seventh source position, deletion of every seventh position, alternating contextual aliases and periodic adjacent swaps. A separate oracle records the exact original index for every query token, null origins for insertions, every deletion and all alias choices. Barriers remain explicit. Public views contain symbolic strings and token offsets; the predictor has no access to true-parent labels or the origin records.

The 191-file code/input seal precedes full corpus generation. **Feature availability is frozen before pair/panel scoring**. All predictions and their hash receipt are saved before oracle metadata is loaded for assessment. The scorer, ten-token window, repeat eligibility and two-shared-type qualification rule are unchanged from v30. No parameter is fitted here.

Availability means the query has at least two eligible fingerprint types, not that those types survive the transformation or identify a parent. All **92 unavailable queries remain** in the 200-query denominator. Their five panel conditions yield 460 unavailable panels, and all 460 abstain. The conditional 24/24 bijective result must therefore be reported alongside the all-case 24/40 result. An abstention on an unavailable query supplies no evidence against the underlying generated relation.

The result confirms the scorer's intended renaming invariance on observable fixtures. It also measures a narrow robustness envelope: regular insertions sharply reduce recovery, deletions and alternating aliases produce no parent qualifications in these fixtures, and the specified local swaps are sometimes tolerated. These fixed edits are not a sampled distribution of paraphrases or FL mechanisms. Their counts do not establish a general language-model ceiling.

## Rejection, ambiguity and the paired-world limit

| Panel condition | All panels | True-parent nominations | Non-parent nominations | Abstentions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Parent plus three distractors | 200 | 48 | 1 | 151 |
| Parent absent, three distractors | 200 | 0 | 1 | 199 |
| Parent and equivalent impostor plus distractors | 200 | 0 | 1 | 199 |
| Equivalent impostor replaces parent | 200 | 0 | 49 | 151 |
| Anchor frame replaces parent | 200 | 0 | 1 | 199 |

The three absent-parent rejection conditions contain **51 non-parent nominations out of 600 panels**, with 549 abstentions. Forty-eight selections name the constructed equivalent impostor. The remaining three rejection errors are repeated appearances of one underlying insertion/shuffle collision, not three independent failure mechanisms. No anchor frame itself is selected; that frame has no eligible fingerprints, while another distractor can still win its panel.

That underlying collision is base p01k29, vocabulary8, insertion query `queryf981a7eb509e626a8f56`. In its present panel, the parent scores zero and the frequency-preserving shuffled distractor in slot2 scores two. The same distractor wins when the parent is absent, replaced by a frame, replaced by the impostor, or accompanied by it. It also causes the single failure to abstain in the explicit collision condition. Exact witnesses and all panel decisions remain in the saved predictions.

All **40 parent/impostor equivalence certificates** verify: each impostor changes unblocked surface strings while preserving the complete fingerprint profile and occurrence counts. All **200 paired present/impostor-only panels** have identical slot-level feature observations, pairwise scores and decisions. In **48 pairs**, slot0 is nominated in both: one panel contains the recorded generating parent, while the other contains a constructed non-parent under the recorded key/lineage.

This is a representation-level non-identifiability demonstration. It concerns equal fingerprint observations, not identical raw text or every possible method. The impostor is intentionally constructed, not independently sampled natural material. A different unknown renaming key could explain it as well. Consequently, changing a threshold cannot separate those equal-feature panels while retaining a nomination in one of them. Explicit co-present ties often trigger abstention; a missing equivalent alternative cannot be recognized from these features alone.

## Frozen criteria and verification

| Candidate-localization requirement | Observed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| All available bijective paired positives qualify | 24/24 | Pass |
| All available bijective present panels recover parent | 24/24 | Pass |
| All 600 rejection panels abstain | 549/600 | Fail |
| All 200 explicit collision panels abstain | 199/200 | Fail |
| All unavailable panels abstain | 460/460 | Pass |

The combined candidate-localization criterion fails. Engineering calibration completes: all 1,000 cases are scored, all 40 profile-equivalence certificates agree, and all 200 paired-world features/scores/decisions agree. These outcomes are distinct from natural scientific acceptance, which remains null.

The independent verifier imports no primary v31 generator, predictor or assessment code. It reconstructs the generator and oracle through separate origin/alias calculations, uses the independent v30 pairwise-equality formulation, and replays every public view, raw token span, feature count, witness, panel score, origin outcome, metric and gate. It checks prediction-before-oracle staging, input/code/output hashes and preservation of all 30 prior runs/seals. The [verification receipt](000031_verification.json) passes, including the failed admission criterion.

No FL/source text, PDF, semantic dictionary, external model, network or new source is used by this assay. Generator keys/code are public; these are known-truth synthetic fixtures, not secret held-out language trials. No natural source identity, semantic label, independent review or untouched-test unit is admitted. Natural semantic metrics remain null.

## Stopping boundary and next experiment

Stop the pattern-only localization branch at this result. Preserve the fixed fixtures, unavailable cases, hard negatives and failed criteria. Do not shorten windows or tune a threshold to remove these errors, and do not use the conditional bijective success as permission to rescore the natural FL pairs.

The next bounded experiment should add a genuinely new constraint: **learn and freeze a symbol mapping from separate synthetic training passages, then test its transfer on new passages under the same generated key**, with a gap-aware alignment rule and explicit unknown-form abstention. Keep generator truth out of prediction, and include the equivalent impostors, absent-parent panels and unseen-key controls. An independently frozen cross-passage mapping can supply information that within-paragraph equality patterns discard; a more permissive pattern score alone cannot resolve the paired-world ambiguity.

That would test synthetic transfer and edit handling, with training/test passages and keys declared before scoring. It would still provide no natural FL meanings or held-out linguistic validation. Returning to natural semantic claims requires independently supported correspondences and a separate registration, not another interpretation of v30's failed targets.
