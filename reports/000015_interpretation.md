Audit interpretation and evidence boundaries

This is a completed package and available-evidence audit. It is not a certification of all proposed translations. The public download supplies clean reading editions, whereas the public post describes research alignment files and confidence-marked records. Those descriptions do not make missing per-unit confidence, original captures or occurrence/edition references available in this archive. The preserved [public claim and download discovery](../data/v15/discovery/public_package_link.json) and [author inventory](https://www.reddit.com/r/ForgottenLanguages/comments/1v1q1fw/i_translated_all_24_kamri_posts_on_forgotten/) record that distinction.

Of the 55 units corroborated in both textual views, 41 English matches are literal saved-text occurrences and 14 require ordered-word normalization, which ignores punctuation and case but preserves every word in sequence. One is flagged as short text; none of the scoped occurrences is repeated within its individual saved document. Multiple historical snapshots of the same post are evidence copies, not independent trials. The report's English total includes qualified alchemical/Gad sources and unreviewed Christisis OCR; only the separate authoritative-source counts pass the source/extraction screen. No scored accuracy, false-admission rate or confidence estimate is derived from these counts.

The source-order supplement checks all thirteen article/source combinations with scoped English matches in one consistent normalized view. The matched subsets contain zero adjacent order inversions. Skipped units and absent sources remain unverified, so this does not establish the author's complete ordered-alignment claim. The repeated units in article 15 are short dialogue: units 8/40/43 and 9/41. They remain in the denominator and are explicitly grouped in the duplicate ledger.

An example of substantive editorial change is article 12, unit 1: its proposed English replaces the source's magic terminology with hypermemetic terminology. The full unit therefore receives no literal-source-match credit. Such changes need separate editorial correspondence review rather than relaxed matching. Western's 48 differing English renderings similarly require original-passage review before any word-position comparison. The audit makes no claim that such readings are necessarily wrong.

All 143 frozen input files, execution code and run outputs are hash-bound. The independent verifier re-extracts labels directly from every reading edition, replays all occurrence searches and offset spans, checks wrong-component and wrong-article exclusions, and verifies acquisition bytes and all fourteen historical manifests. The 111-test suite includes new checks for archive traversal, duplicate names, executables, symlinks, locator removal, Western language labels and literal occurrence boundaries. PDF image review and source authority assessments are disclosed single-analyst inputs; the automated verifier cannot independently certify those judgments.
