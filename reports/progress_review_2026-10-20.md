# Progress review after experiment 60

This is a planning review, not experiment 61, a new registration, or a rescore.
The saved reports and inventories were checked on 2026-10-20. No research
network request or candidate admission is part of this review.

The project has a strong evidence pipeline and a narrow, reproducible natural
case study. It has not yet demonstrated natural-language decipherment. The
number of completed experiments overstates the number of independent scientific
advances: much of the recent sequence resolves acquisition, parser, provenance
and accounting dependencies around the same exposed ANITA example.

## Most consequential design improvement

A comparator must have enough numeric overlap to challenge the claimed score.
The E58 minimum of three shared types is a feasibility screen, not sufficient
protection against a trivial reference-versus-comparator gap. For any fixed
window, numeric LCS cannot exceed the multiset intersection count with the
target. If that upper bound is below six, the window cannot tie the reference,
regardless of its order.

The unchanged E56 target has ten numeric occurrences and nine types; only 180
appears twice. A candidate sharing exactly three types can therefore match at
most four target occurrences. Such a candidate could pass the old three-type
screen while remaining incapable of challenging a score of six. This is a
bound derived from the frozen inventory, not a new LCS computation.

For a separately registered future direct specificity challenge, require at
least one source window under the unchanged eleven-anchor cap to have numeric
bag capacity at least six. Preserve all candidates failing that capacity check
in the acquisition denominator. Capacity is only a necessary condition: keep
bag overlap and order-sensitive results separate, retain every scanned window
and permutation control, and do not infer source identity from either. Ideally
competitors should also have comparable topic, period and scope. The original
three-type rule and E56/E58 outcomes remain immutable.

## Reduce procedural overhead and impose a stopping point

Complete the already proposed offline attribution audit of E60's saved page.
Then propose one final, fixed two-candidate intake/challenge for this discovery
branch. Freeze nomination order, exposure exclusions, native checks, the new
capacity diagnostic, request/conditional-alias limits and comparison rules before
that batch. Eligible captured candidates can proceed through capture validation
and the frozen comparison as phases of one bounded experiment. Routine helper
checks need not become separate scientific milestones.

Do not keep extending searches if that batch supplies no comparator capable of
challenging six. Close this route/cohort as feasibility-limited and retain the
current association as an exposed case study. This would not show that suitable
comparators are absent from the wider archive. If a capable comparator is found,
report the challenge outcome, including ties or failures, and close the batch.
Even a favorable result remains case-specific and nonsemantic.

For the broader decipherment project, further expansion should depend on a new
kind of evidence: a defensible independent review route or fresh source-group
material that supports predictions on opaque text held out before development.
A second model checking our files does not automatically meet that requirement.
Without this evidence, additional architecture or polished translations will
not resolve the central uncertainty.

Use the existing automated manifests, archive checkpointing and fixtures to
preserve rigor. Prefer reusable checks for common acquisition/accounting
contracts over duplicated per-experiment scaffolding, while leaving every sealed
historical implementation untouched. Report research progress by claims tested
and falsified, not test count or experiment number.

The recommended immediate step is still the offline result-attribution audit.
This review proposes tighter later criteria and a branch budget; it does not
silently amend a frozen protocol or authorize new research requests.
