# Experiment 11: incorrect anchors and prediction-matching lures

Completed and independently verified on 2026-10-01. **The v10 content-capacity result does not survive this broader challenge family under its narrow admission policy.** Content scoring still admits 256/384 true sources against fully renamed negatives, including deliberately incorrect predictions, but admits many prediction-matching wrong sources. Calibration on both families eliminates those admissions by refusing every fresh case. No model or lexical meaning is promoted.

## Frozen design and execution

The [registration](../experiments/kamri_conflict_v11.md), [configuration](../experiments/kamri_conflict_v11.json) and [benchmark](../benchmarks/kamri_conflict_v11.json) were frozen before the single final execution, `000011_kamri_anchor_conflicts`. There are 768 calibration conditions from seeds 11401–11432 and 768 fresh conditions from seeds 11501–11532. Each panel contains 32 independent worlds, with 24 paired conditions per world: six query settings, full/half source availability and two negative families. Twelve unchanged real queries remain development diagnostics; ten calibrate real thresholds and two extra Misinfons blocks do not. There is no fresh natural source work.

The six settings are a clean query without roster words, a clean three-word query, one/all roster forms cyclically changed per statement, and one/all made unknown. Changes affect six or eighteen query occurrences. The provisional world/lord/made roster, prediction policy, both editorial backgrounds, all four scoring modes, search, decoder and minimum three linked atoms are inherited unchanged from v10. Original English words and occurrence identities remain fixed across perturbations. Only source availability removes a reference; incorrect or omitted predictions never leave its denominator.

Each candidate pool has four distinct texts. Primary negatives rename every nonfunction word, and remain fixed across corruption settings. Stress negatives contain a lure whose known content follows the expected emitted predictions, plus two cyclic variants. Unknown content is renamed and functions are preserved. Lures are generated by fixed rules rather than selected by scores. Every corrupted occurrence, expected prediction, available identity, candidate role and agreement count is saved. No truth metadata enters scoring.

All four modes share each candidate's base alignment and decoder within a query/background. Across corruption settings, the input changes and the base path or decoder can change. That distinction matters for the token effects below. There was one final execution, no replacements or tuning, and every registered condition is retained. The run took approximately 120 seconds.

## Why the stress controls fail

With clean three-word predictions, the true source and first lure have the same ordered known content, while their unknown content differs. Both content modes tie and refuse all 64 source-present cases. Removing the true source makes the ordered-content lure uniquely best: it is falsely admitted in **64/64 clean absent controls**. Bag scoring keeps the cyclic lure variants tied and refuses them. Good content-order agreement therefore does not identify a source work or establish source presence.

Changing one anchor per statement makes the first lure uniquely best and admitted in all 64 cases in both content modes. Changing all three cyclically preserves the bag of roster words: bag scoring ties and refuses all 64; ordered scoring chooses the first lure and admits all 64. The advantage of order against reordered correct predictions in v10 becomes a disadvantage when predictions themselves are wrong.

One omission produces another 40 wrong admissions in each content mode: 25/32 with full sources and 15/32 with half sources. The missing words no longer enter the content evidence denominator, so the true source and first lure can both have quality 1. The retained base scorer then chooses the lure. For example, seed 11501/full has original-background base strengths 0.265 for the first lure and 0.260 for the true source, with content-order quality 1 for both. Their selected final block merges statements 4–5; its structural/lexical similarity is 0.985 for the lure versus 0.970 for the true source. The occurrence-specific unknown replacements in the lure better match the perturbed query's repetition structure. This is inherited search/scoring behavior, not a new content-order distinction. All 104 bag and 168 ordered wrong admissions select the designated first lure.

All omissions leave no roster evidence. Both content modes give every candidate zero, retain all 64 stress cases and refuse them. Such refusal is not positive identification evidence. Across all fresh conditions, content modes have 256 all-zero ties; nominal ID tie-breaking remains in the saved metrics and never counts as unique retrieval.

## Correct-source token effects

Even conditional on supplying the true source, all-conflict anchors harm the inherited decoder. All modes recover all **3,456/3,456 available statement edges**, but token identities differ:

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| zero | 5768/5768 | 5768 | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% |
| clean | 5768/5768 | 5768 | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% |
| conflict_one | 5192/5768 | 5192 | 0 | 100.00% | 90.01% |
| conflict_all | 4040/5768 | 5192 | 1152 | 77.81% | 70.04% |
| omit_one | 5768/5768 | 5768 | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% |
| omit_all | 5768/5768 | 5768 | 0 | 100.00% | 100.00% |

One conflict drops the incorrect occurrence rather than pairing it: 576 correct identities are lost across both families/extents, with no extras. All-conflict drops 1,728 correct identities and introduces 1,152 cross-occurrence pairs. Omission preserves positional token recovery in this artificial schema, including the omitted identities; this reflects the decoder's positional treatment of unknowns and does not recover or validate their meanings. All modes share these conditional counts. Overall conditional token recall is 32,304/34,608 (93.34%) and precision is 32,304/33,456 (96.56%).

The saved 4,096 corruption-minus-clean contrasts preserve the paired world/extent/family/variant. Ordered-content wrong admissions rise by 64 for one conflict, 64 for all conflicts and 40 for one omission. Clean absent errors already exist: conflict contrasts add zero absent errors, one omission removes 24 and all omissions remove 64, because rejection replaces evidence. These sums are descriptive paired counts, not independent statistical successes.

## Broader calibration and real diagnostics

Primary content thresholds are 0.000001 because renamed calibration negatives have no roster matches. Base/positional thresholds are approximately 0.265001 in the original background and 0.205001 in the tolerant background. Pooled thresholds across both families are those respective larger values for every mode. Both policies were sealed before any fresh/audit scoring. Pooled calibration refuses **all 768/768 fresh cases in all eight variants**, giving zero correct admissions, zero wrong admissions and zero absent false admissions. This is universal refusal, not useful general rejection reliability. Thresholds and data remain fixed; no policy is selected as a winner.

Every real candidate ranking, path, score and admission decision exactly matches v10 for all eight modes under both policies. Base/positional uniquely retrieve all ten development queries and admit nine. Content modes uniquely retrieve and admit five, with five zero-score ties; all Misinfons content scores remain zero. The two additional audit blocks are not fresh natural validation. Prior unavailable sources and their historical denominators remain preserved.

## Interpretation and next step

This controlled test establishes sensitivity to wrong anchors and known-evidence-matched lures in one artificial schema. It does not measure natural anchor error frequency, validate the roster, establish register transfer, or estimate archive-wide source rejection. Primary capacity remains narrow; improving that point estimate would not solve the clean source-absence ambiguity. No lexical entry or scoring model is promoted.

The next useful milestone is an acquisition-first independent natural validation pilot: find an auditable additional source work, freeze a work-level holdout and reviewed occurrence references before judging lexical predictions, and include source-absent and known-evidence-matched negatives. Count unavailable acquisitions and unsupported occurrences explicitly. If no eligible new work can be acquired, report that bounded result without substituting inspected development material. Separate source retrieval, positional correspondence and lexical meaning throughout.
