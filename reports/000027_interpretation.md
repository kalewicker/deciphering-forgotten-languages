## Literal rule and extraction limits

Four-token seeds require exact NFC/casefolded token equality, no blocked token, at least three distinct forms and at least two words with four or more alphabetic characters. Each matching seed is extended maximally while residual tokens agree; every distinct maximal aligned run and its exact query/source quote and offsets are retained. Qualification requires at least eight tokens. This threshold was frozen before execution and is not a calibrated statistical or semantic criterion. Shared shorter words, ineligible four-grams or nonliteral correspondences are not ruled out by an empty eligible seed set.

The source pages are new, page-separated derivatives of the unchanged publisher PDFs, extracted with pinned pypdf 6.10.0 and rendered with PDFium 156.0.8076.0. Text is retained raw, including headings, citations, page labels, line breaks, split words and footnotes. No spelling, citation or boundary repair occurs. Page boundaries are hard candidate boundaries; punctuation/newlines inside a page are ignored by token matching while their raw quotes remain available. Footnote/line-wrap artifacts still limit interpretation of any apparent match.

All eight complete page images were inspected: text, headings, printed page labels and footnotes are legible. Western has page-edge main-text and footnote continuations. Risk's page-2 footnote interrupts a main-text continuation into page 3, and the page views retain numbered citations and split words. This is same-assistant layout/extraction QA, not independent semantic review. The previous packet extracts remain unchanged. Their three legacy-view control scores are also zero, so this specific negative result agrees across those two saved representations; that agreement does not prove extraction or coverage completeness.

Exact query-context copies are eligible positives despite the unknown text because literal equality is observable without interpreting it. All three pass. The ordered anchor-only frames from v26 fail after masking, showing that this diagnostic excludes the earlier anchor-only signal. Reverse and legacy views are fixed descriptive controls, not alternative successful criteria. The positive copies deliberately contain FL material and cannot establish an immediate publisher source or word meaning.

## Verification and stopping boundary

All **210 tests pass**, covering threshold sensitivity, masking barriers, repetitive/short-word seed rejection, Unicode/raw offset integrity, repeated matches, fixed contexts/windows and order. The execution matcher indexes and extends four-token seeds; the independent verifier enumerates matching runs along alignment diagonals. They agree on all 2,325 natural scores, twelve control views, all maximal-match records, rankings, raw spans and decisions. Independent PDF replay regenerates all eight page texts and RGB pixel arrays. It also validates all 26 prior run inventories and deliverable hashes. The [verification receipt](000027_verification.json) passes, including the failed scientific gate.

The registered result is expected-page retrieval 0/3, source-work-absent nominations 0/3, anchor-frame qualifications 0/3 and exact-context positive qualifications 3/3. The first required gate component fails; the other three pass. No extraction method, context size, score, mask, threshold, source panel or outcome was changed after scoring. Registration, code/input seal, extracted pages, visual QA, frozen panel and benchmark precede execution in the saved chronology.

Experiments 1–26 and all original packets remain unchanged. Source identities, semantic labels, independent-review admissions and untouched-test admissions remain **zero**; semantic evaluation metrics are **null**. No network acquisition, target-gap rescore, dictionary induction or translation model occurs. The exposed automated report/appendix remains a lead rather than independent linguistic evidence. Earlier gap alternatives, copied-anchor failures and source/provenance limits remain intact.

Experiment 27 stops here. The next useful bounded step is a **block-level boundary and coverage audit** using the existing raw archived HTML and source-page layout: distinguish article paragraphs, quotations, bibliography/navigation and PDF main text/footnotes without forcing FL-to-English alignment. Preserve ambiguous segments and original offsets in a versioned mapping. A later nonliteral correspondence test would need a separately specified, independently assessable prediction; this literal-context failure supplies no meanings and should not be converted into one by relaxing the rule.
