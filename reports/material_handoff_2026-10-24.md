# Material handoff to resume confirmation

Prepared 2026-10-24. This is a resource handoff, not a new experiment,
registration, capture, review or research result. E84 remains the latest completed
step. E79's model stays fixed and unconfirmed on new material.

We can resume when a documented material source becomes available. The smallest
useful contribution is **one saved native Kamri listing**, or an authorized copy
of the exact archival record below. Do not collect four favorite posts first:
selection and a complete budget must be fixed before opening candidate material
for modeling. A listing supplies nomination metadata, not confirmed test units.

## If the native site opens in your browser

Open the observed [Kamri listing](https://forgottenlanguages-full.forgottenlanguages.org/search/label/Kamri)
and save it with the browser's page-save function. Preserve the original saved
file and any companion files without editing or pasting its text into a new file.
Record the requested URL, final address-bar URL, save time and exact save method.
A browser snapshot may represent a rendered DOM rather than raw HTTP bytes;
record the representation without claiming lossless original-response capture.

Place the files under the local-only `data/incoming/` directory, or keep them
elsewhere and provide their absolute paths. Copy the
[blank material-offer template](../experiments/material_offer_template_2026-10-24.json)
next to them and fill in facts you know. Leave unknown facts null. The template
is descriptive and does not have to be complete for an initial provenance review.
A screenshot or pasted excerpt can be a lead, but does not replace the saved
representation needed for byte/offset provenance.

If you already have saved pages, preserve their existing bytes and selection
history. Record whether anyone selected them using source matches, model results
or perceived ease, and who has already inspected them. Their availability can be
assessed, but they will not automatically be labeled untouched confirmation data.

## What happens after material is offered

First hash and preserve the offered bytes, and document provenance, capture
method, prior exposure and selection history. Freeze a separate intake protocol
before parsing candidate content or selecting a cohort. Then assess identity,
representation, duplicate/connected-group evidence, masks and capacity without
model scoring. A supplied archive record must be checked as a record, rather
than normalized until it matches a digest.

Only a separately registered confirmation design can score eligible reserved
material with E79's fixed rule. The old 13 groups cannot be relabeled new, and
four new URLs cannot be assumed to be four independent groups. Raw captures and
notes remain off public main under [PUBLICATION.md](../PUBLICATION.md).
The separate independently reviewed semantic benchmark remains 0/4.

If neither resource is available, confirmation stays unlaunched. No further TLS,
archive-endpoint, digest-normalization or model-parameter sweep is pending.
