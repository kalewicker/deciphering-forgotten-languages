# Experiment 30: paragraph repetition patterns fail the localization criterion

The anchor-masked repetition-pattern diagnostic **fails its registered structural-development criterion**. None of the three queries uniquely retrieves its nominated source paragraph; all nominated paragraphs score zero. Western names instead selects the preceding paragraph, Risk spellings selects a Western fragment, and Western Saturn has no eligible fingerprints at all. The opaque-renaming sensitivity controls qualify for **2/3**, leaving Saturn's abstention uninformative under this feature rule.

All **243 tests pass**. Independent reconstruction agrees on the complete registry, all 108 natural scores, all controls, every one of 199 shuffled-panel searches and all **21,492 null scores**. Engineering replay passes while the structural criterion remains failed. No source identity, meaning, semantic accuracy, independent review or untouched admission is established. All 29 prior runs, original packets and earlier failed gates remain unchanged.

## Fixed units and prediction

The frozen registry retains all **55 FL paragraph runs** and **65 source regions**, partitioning all 293 source layout lines exactly once. Western uses the unchanged v28 geometry text; Risk uses the unchanged v29 character derivative. Every new source-unit string links its lines and synthetic joins back to the saved parent JSON/line IDs. Existing coordinate systems remain distinct. All headings, notes, margins, metadata and page-edge fragments remain in the registry, even when excluded from the declared candidate panel.

The primary panel has **36 candidate units: 22 Western and 14 Risk**. They comprise paragraphs, quotations, list items, a prose lead and explicitly incomplete page fragments. Same-assistant source-only visual segmentation is exposed editorial evidence, not independent semantic labeling. Page-edge pieces are not joined or repaired. The three queries remain selected observations in only **two source works**, with the Western queries dependent.

These source nominations are exposed anchor-location leads. They do not establish a one-to-one paragraph alignment. In particular, one FL paragraph might combine material from several source paragraphs; the registered single-unit target remains a restriction to test rather than a fact to assume.

The structural prediction uses a mathematical invariant: consistent one-to-one token renaming preserves the equality pattern within a token window. Ten-token windows are canonicalized by first-occurrence IDs, independent of word strings. Eligibility requires at least four distinct forms and two forms repeated at least twice. The score counts distinct shared eligible fingerprint types, and qualification requires at least two. All nineteen v26 anchor-component forms are hard barriers globally. No literal name, word length, proposed gloss, learned lexicon or embedding contributes to the score.

The corrected tails compare each observed **whole-panel maximum** with the maxima of 199 whole shuffled panels, using `(1 + null maxima >= observed)/200`. The names observation has two null maxima equal to three and none greater; Risk has 25 maxima at least three, including 22 exact ties. Saturn has 199 zero ties. None meets the frozen <=0.01 criterion. All unit scores, top ties and null permutations remain saved, including zero-score candidates; no permutation was excluded as an identity draw, and the saved draws happen to contain none at the unit level.

The shuffles preserve unblocked token histograms within each source unit and keep anchor barriers fixed. They condition on this exposed segmentation, mask, convenience panel and query selection. Their tails are descriptive conditional diagnostics, not population significance, semantic accuracy or a real-world false-match rate.

The Western names match consists of **three overlapping ten-token windows within one twelve-token span**: query token positions [30,42), source u02 positions [37,49). The source span begins “a concept of God. Actually, one discovers a vision of a purely”. Thus three fingerprint types do not represent three independent passages. This is a local structural lead in the same source neighborhood, outside the registered target paragraph. It is compatible with a paragraph-merge explanation, but that is a post-result hypothesis; u02 is not substituted for the frozen u03 target and the gate remains failed. No induced word pairing is admitted as a meaning.

Risk's top score also comes from three overlapping windows within twelve-token spans, query [33,45) and Western u01 [60,72). This example demonstrates that the same kind of short repeated-word pattern can nominate a different work in this panel. The other work is an operational alternative, not independently certified ground-truth unrelated material; this result does not establish a population false-positive rate or exclude mixed-source reuse.

Saturn's zero is a **feature-availability failure**: after the fixed mask and eligibility rule, it has no eligible query patterns. An exact opaque renaming also scores zero. Its natural abstention therefore supplies no useful evidence against the nominated relationship, stable substitution in general, or nonliteral reuse. It stays in the complete three-query denominator rather than being dropped or given a shorter window.

## Controls and all failed criterion components

Opaque consistent renamings score **3, 0 and 6** for names, Saturn and Risk respectively. Names and Risk qualify, and each opaque control is uniquely nominated when inserted into its source-work-absent panel. These deliberately engineered collisions show the invariant cannot distinguish a natural source from a constructed repetition-equivalent text. They are sensitivity controls and an identifiability limit, not linguistic positives.

All three anchor-only frames score zero. Names and Saturn abstain in the frame-augmented source-work-absent panel; Risk still nominates Western u01, exactly as it does without the frame. The failed frame-panel component is therefore an existing natural-panel nomination, **not the copied-anchor frame being selected**.

Removing all nominated-work units leaves **one nomination out of three**: Risk selects Western u01. Names and Saturn abstain. Reversing each query yields top scores 1, 0 and 3; only reversed Risk still nominates Western u01. These controls remain descriptive and are not chosen as an alternative criterion after execution.

The combined criterion fails on every required component. This does not reject translation, paraphrase, context-dependent/many-to-one substitutions, reordering or the broader source hypotheses. It rejects use of this frozen pattern-only localization rule as sufficient support on this panel. The sensitivity failure and unknown-negative status limit even that interpretation.

## Verification and stopping boundary

Registration, boundary review and the 186-file code/input seal precede preparation. The complete registry, synthetic controls, 199 keyed permutations and benchmark are frozen before the single scoring run. All eight unchanged page images were inspected for source-unit boundaries. No source boundary, target, feature width, mask, threshold, null horizon or representation changes after scoring.

The separate verifier imports no primary v30 code. It independently rebuilds Unicode token spans and the registry, checks every source-line partition/control/permutation, and computes window equivalence using pairwise token-equality partitions instead of the execution code's renaming map. It reproduces all raw fingerprint witnesses, natural/control decisions, null unit scores, maxima, exact tail counts, gates and report accounting. All 29 prior manifest inventories and deliverable hashes verify. The [receipt](000030_verification.json) passes, including the failed structural criterion.

No network, source-page expansion, model, dictionary induction, old-target rescore or external message occurs. Scientific acceptance and semantic metrics remain null; source identities, semantic labels, independent-review admissions and untouched-test admissions remain zero.

Experiment 30 stops here. Do not merge the neighboring paragraph into a new target, shorten the window for Saturn, weaken the mask or revise the threshold to turn this archive into success. The pattern-only branch has shown both insufficient sensitivity and conditional specificity failures.

The next useful bounded step is a **separately registered mechanism-calibration assay with known synthetic transformations and fixed hard negatives**, before another natural-source localization attempt. Measure feature availability before scoring while retaining every case in the relevant denominator; cover consistent substitution, controlled insertions/deletions, context-dependent forms, local reordering and repetition-equivalent impostors. Require the method to expose non-identifiability and abstain where the observed features cannot distinguish alternatives. This can assess an alignment method against known generated truth; it cannot create semantic gold for these natural FL pairs. Preserve the Western neighboring-paragraph lead as a proposal for later prediction or review, not a renamed success.
