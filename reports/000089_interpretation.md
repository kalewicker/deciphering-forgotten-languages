# E89: a documented Yid archive offer is available as an intake lead

The single registered primary-report check found an explicit **Dropbox ZIP offer**
in the [Yid report](https://www.reddit.com/r/ForgottenLanguages/comments/1v2be1i/all_86_yid_posts_from_forgotten_languages/).
The author describes it as covering **86 translated Yid posts**. That count and
the translations are claims, not verified corpus contents or reference answers.
The archive itself was **not downloaded**. Payload availability, file integrity,
provenance and independent-group capacity remain unchecked.

This is a concrete new resource lead for a different development direction. It
is not confirmation of E79's Kamri model, a validated translation, or an admitted
semantic benchmark work. The natural semantic benchmark remains **0/4**.

E88 completed the exact-overlap audit. E85/E87's repeated Kamri listing collection

context are retained with exact Unicode code-point spans in the local ledger.
No comments, related-post navigation or third-party claims were used as corpus
validation. No link was followed, no contact made, and no payload inspected.

## Representation and uncertainty

The retained 23,460-byte artifact is a serialization of the complete web-provider
result available to the agent, wrapped with return UTC and a representation label.
It is **not raw origin HTML** or an authenticated native FL capture. The provider
indicates a relative cache age of “last month”; no exact origin capture time is
inferred. Registration, request reservation and provider return times are distinct.
The tool may perform internal retrieval/redirect operations; one tool call is not
a verified count of underlying HTTP transactions.

The linked ZIP can later be inspected as a third-party research package. Its
filenames, claimed language and English renderings will not authenticate native
FL bytes or establish semantics. Prior payload exposure is unknown; claims and
examples in the public report were read during this step. No blind-status claim
is made. Yid is a separate declared label, so its availability would support a
new resource/development question rather than same-language Kamri confirmation.

## Next bounded step and stop

E89 stops here, having found a documented offer. The next justified step is a
separately registered **single archive acquisition and metadata inventory** of
the documented offer, with the retrieval URL and permitted redirects fixed
before acquisition. Fix the size/time budget, preserve response bytes, check
archive safety/duplicates and account for all members before content selection. Missing, blocked, oversized or malformed material must remain a failed
intake, with no alternative link or replacement selected after the result.

That future step should inventory the package before opening translations for
model use. Claimed translations and lexicons stay quarantined as hypotheses;
file count cannot substitute for source-group independence. No extraction,
training, source adjudication, cross-language scoring or semantic admission is
launched by this report. E77's failed gate, E79's development result, E88's audit
and all prior acquisition failures remain unchanged.
