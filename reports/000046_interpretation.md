# Experiment 46: masked numeric order separates the fixed order controls

The masked target and publisher material share **six distinct literal numeric
keys in order**. Their numeric LCS is **6**, versus a maximum of **5** across
all 34 predeclared bag-preserving order controls. Functional checks pass, so

Same-topic alternative source documents remain untested by this control family.

are masked. Paragraphs with zero anchors remain in the control inventory.
The [scope ledger](../data/v46/scope.json) preserves every mask and unit.

The extractor scans original text and then excludes masked/out-of-scope spans,

lexemes and uppercase Latin runs retain exact spelling and their original

fragments and mixed-case/number identifiers are excluded by the registered rule.
No quantities, units or acronym meanings are inferred. In particular, **100.000
and 100,000 remain different keys**, as do 2007 and 7.
