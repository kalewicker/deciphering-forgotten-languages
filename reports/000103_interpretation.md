# E103 fixes one Yid localization lead and records a blocked source request

The first eligible ordinal in E102's 28-row pool was **candidate 04**. It has
30 proposed English blocks. Its external claim cites the Flowers, Plants, Fruits,
and Trees chapter of Manly P. Hall's *The Secret Teachings of All Ages*. The
selection used catalog metadata. The ordinal and its catalog component are
reserved from pairing-rule development. [Registered protocol](../experiments/yid_localization_v103.md)

The single registered request for the exact cited chapter returned **HTTP 403**.
The archive preserves the 5,689-byte response, headers and hashed request receipt.
The response failed the source intake gate. Edition identity, whitespace matches,
token matches and passage order are all unmeasured. The denominator remains
30 proposed blocks. There were zero pairing runs and zero admitted replication
panels. The experiment's 22-span and 80% localization thresholds remain untested.

The choice of lowest eligible ordinal and first catalog HTTPS locator put
source selection ahead of result inspection. The single-request budget limits
this finding to that request and representation. A separately documented copy
of the exact cited chapter would permit a new intake for the already selected
lead. E103 stops at the recorded access failure.

Replay independently checked the 28-row pool, selected ordinal and locator,
registration timing, request and response hashes, and null comparison fields.
The request records remain local. With the local archive, run
`.venv/bin/python scripts/verify_yid_localization_v103.py`.
