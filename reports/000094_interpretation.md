## What this changes and where to stop

E93 established correspondence for one Armenian-script example. E94 independently
executes the frozen comparator on the other two development samples and corroborates
their supplied text representations, including the longer diacritic-rich sample.
This is new representation evidence, not an independent semantic review or a
corpus-wide accuracy estimate. Package normalization may still matter elsewhere;
these results do not prove all 3,485 pairs are faithful or meaningful.

E94 is complete and stopped. More page collection is not the immediate priority.
The next useful bounded question is the source basis of these already corroborated
examples: register an audit of their existing catalog claims and whether those
claims actually specify per-unit evidence, distinguish claimed source-aligned
wording from reconstruction, and support a concrete later falsification test.
Do not infer missing evidence labels from fluent English or promote catalog claims
to verified sources. Any source retrieval or adjudication needs a separate fixed
budget and protocol. Independent review remains a separate unresolved requirement.

benchmark admission were launched.
