## Capacity decision

The v36 registration fixes a minimum pilot capacity of four new independent
connected works: two development and two test, each with at least two reviewed,
nonoverlapping passage units and hash-bound FL/source artifacts. Count in the
audited inventory: **0/4 qualifying works**. The old v14 candidate inventory also
reports no remaining unexposed components. Relabeling old passages or splitting
one book into more articles cannot fill this gap.

Four works would enable only a small pilot. It would not supply statistical
power, calibrated semantic confidence or a general decipherment claim. Source
relationship, passage correspondence, lexical prediction and translation still
need separate judgments and metrics.

## Next bounded task

Prepare and execute a separately registered **source-first acquisition-feasibility
pilot** before building another alignment feature. A useful registration would:

1. Fix a small acquisition budget and a metadata-only candidate selection rule.
   Record every failed request and rejected candidate. Do not pick a replacement
   because a match looks easier. Avoid importing proposed translations or
   dictionaries as reference answers.
2. Check candidate works against all exposed source components, editions,
   mirrors and composite connections. Save each selection's exposure history;
   previously inspected material can serve development, with its status explicit.
3. Obtain lossless dated FL and publisher/source artifacts with exact edition,
   coverage, hashes and extraction offsets. Check paragraph and page boundaries,
   including omitted or substituted material. Source availability alone is not
   correspondence evidence.
4. Establish a concrete review route. Review can conclude supported, rejected
   or uncertain, and must identify its author, role and prior exposure. A fresh
   automated comparison may produce useful proposals; describe its exposure and
   limitations, and do not count it as independent semantic acceptance by default.
   No available independent reviewer remains an explicit unresolved requirement.
5. Before model evaluation, reserve connected test works and withhold their
   correspondence/lexical answer keys from the prediction process. Select realistic
   near-neighbor and source-absent candidate panels independently of predicted
   success. Freeze predictions before opening answers, and retain wrong calls,
   unknowns and every denominator.

The first outcome to seek is **a verified capacity ledger**, with enough qualified
new works or a precise failure explaining why the registered acquisition/review
budget could not produce them. Acquisition failure and uncertain correspondence
are legitimate outcomes. Do not start a natural lexical or translation benchmark
until its evidence requirements are met.

Experiment 36 itself made no new requests, external contacts, natural target
scores or semantic admissions. Its synthetic failure and all earlier failures
remain preserved. The old data can continue supporting development work, but the
next major research investment should resolve new-evidence and review access.
