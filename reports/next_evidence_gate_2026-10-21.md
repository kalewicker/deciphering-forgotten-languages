# Next evidence gate after E75

Decision date: 2026-10-21. This is a planning decision, not E76, a new
registration, an empirical audit or a new research result.

The next useful step is to secure a reviewer who can independently assess a
bounded source/passage relationship. That resource is currently unavailable.
Natural evaluation remains unlaunched. No reviewer has been contacted or
assigned, and there is no pending model run.

## Why this is the next step

[E71](000071_interpretation.md) identified independent passage evidence as the
missing resource. The user subsequently chose Codex review because no human
reviewer was available. [E72](000072_interpretation.md) produced an uncertain
Risk relationship; [E74](000074_interpretation.md) documented supplied Western
literal clues with assistance disclosed. [E75](000075_interpretation.md) then
answered the narrower component question: the reviewing session supported 3/3
genuine literal claims and rejected 2/2 constructed false claims.

Those outcomes are useful, but none supplies independent semantic acceptance.
Another fresh model session, another verification script or a larger version of
the same packet would not by itself supply the missing resource. E75's one-batch
stop applies regardless of its positive result. There is no justified next
natural experiment ready to launch with the currently available evidence.

## Concrete handoff requirements

Before sending a packet, a prospective reviewer should provide:

- Their identity or stable attribution, relevant expertise, and whether the work
  will be human, automated or mixed.
- Prior exposure to FL, the nominated source, these packets, project predictions
  and earlier review returns; any relationship to the project or predictions.
- Whether they can assess a fixed supplied passage window, preserve uncertainty,
  and return exact quotations and original-text offsets with contrary evidence.

These disclosures allow an independence assessment; a human label, a clean
session or a completed form alone does not establish independence. A reviewer
need not promise a positive result or claim to understand opaque FL words.
If this resource becomes available, select one unchanged E25 packet and freeze
a separate intake registration **before** sending it. Do not supply E72/E74/E75
answers, projected translations or proposed opaque glosses as review guidance.
The packet's source disclosure must itself be recorded.

The future registration must bind the packet and text hashes, reviewer exposure,
one first-return budget, deadline, rubric and complete denominator. The return
must distinguish literal overlap, passage correspondence, immediate source
identity and semantic meaning, and identify exact spans, alternatives,
counterevidence and uncertainty. Its acceptance rule must assess the evidence
and independence, not simply JSON validity. Known-truth rejection controls may
check a literal component, but cannot substitute for substantive review.

Do not reopen the same Codex packet review under this proposal. Any materially
different automated method needs a separate falsifiable question and a new
independently testable constraint; changing the session, model or prompt is
insufficient justification by itself.

## Outcomes and stopping rule

| Resource or return | Action |
|---|---|
| No suitable reviewer available | Keep this gate unresolved; launch no natural evaluation. No recurring audit or new experiment number is needed. |
| Reviewer available but independence not defensible | Retain the disclosure; treat any resulting material as assisted development evidence, without independent admission. |
| Independently defensible review returns uncertain, rejects the relationship or is incomplete | Preserve the first return and failure/uncertainty. Do not replace the reviewer or expand the window to obtain a favorable answer under the same registration. |
| Independently defensible review supports a bounded passage relationship | Admit only the supported claim under the future registration. The already exposed packet remains development material. |

Even the last outcome would not produce an untouched work or validate opaque
meanings. The natural benchmark still requires **four new independent connected
works**, two development and two test, each with at least two independently
reviewed nonoverlapping passage units. Current qualifying capacity remains
**0/4**. This proposal does not relax that requirement or count the two existing
dossiers as new groups.

## What remains closed

E62 and E70 remain closed feasibility-limited routes. Synthetic feature expansion
remains stopped after the registered failure. No new searches, acquisitions,
natural rescores, reviewer-control batches or expanded packet windows are
authorized by this planning note. Historical protocols, failed gates and
denominators remain intact.

The practical improvement is to make continuation depend on an evidence resource
that can change the scientific conclusion. Until that resource or a materially
different justified proposal exists, further experiment numbering would overstate
progress. This note and the README update add no generated research evidence.
