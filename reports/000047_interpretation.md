# Experiment 47: publisher numeric scaffold exceeds both captured-paper windows

The publisher's sealed numeric LCS of **6** exceeds the strongest 11-numeric-
anchor window in each of the two captured cited papers: **1** for *Neutrino
Radar* and **2** for the ANITA anomalous-events paper. The registered descriptive
specificity criterion passes for this fixed pair of exposed comparators.

This strengthens the limited comparison beyond shuffled versions of one source.
It does not identify the immediate source, establish opaque FL meanings, rule
out other same-topic works or calibrate a probability. These papers are alternative
candidates, not certified false parents or untouched natural test works.

## Frozen scope and equal-budget comparison

The [registration](../experiments/scaffold_specificity_v47.md), config, new
scoring code/tests and 90 input hashes were fixed before comparator extraction
or scoring. The v46 target masks and inventory were reconstructed exactly:
known English quotations, title/header and bibliography stay masked, leaving
the same **10 literal numeric occurrences**. The publisher scope and score
were copied from sealed v46 evidence, without selecting a new publisher passage.

All **8 saved pages** of hep-ph/0209057v1 and **15 saved pages** of
1809.09615v1 were included. Original page text, page IDs, source PDF hashes and
all anchor spans were validated. Pages were joined in saved order with a newline;
each anchor retains its saved page and original page-local character span.
There were **zero network requests and zero new PDF extractions**. The PDFs
and page images were acquired/inspected in earlier steps; this experiment did
not perform a new visual check of individual numeric glyphs or reading order.

The frozen v46 literal extractor was reused without normalization, repair,
quantity interpretation or a selected matching-key list. Unlike the publisher's
14 scoped paragraphs with quoted speech masked, PDF scope includes all headers,
captions, quotations and references without source masks. This gives the papers
broader textual scope and is a disclosed representation asymmetry. It does not
remove PDF extraction, layout or historical-version uncertainty.

Whole-paper scores have unequal exposure, so the primary comparison exhaustively
enumerated every contiguous **11-numeric-anchor** window, stride one, matching
the publisher's numeric occurrence count. This equalizes anchor budget, not text
length, information content or document genre. Every start, score and tie is
retained, including windows crossing page boundaries. No sampled windows,
padding, deduplication or favorable tie breaking were used.
