# Experiment 14: source-first feasibility and the remaining Kamri component

The registered one-component feasibility experiment is complete. **One English opening and one FL opening were acquired, but zero of one selected pairs is reference eligible.** No predictions, permutations, controls or model scores were executed. Accuracy, rejection rates and lexical recovery remain unavailable. No meaning or model is promoted.

This stage differs from the earlier missing-source outcomes: an inspectable, edition-supported source opening was acquired first. [Cambridge's chapter page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/religious-architecture/religious-architecture-anthropological-perspectives/74C1F8701430274A4896ABE463379B96) identifies Oskar Verkaaik, Amsterdam University Press, the 2013 print edition and pages 7–24, and provides 380 words of opening prose. The [JSTOR volume record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt6wp6sx) corroborates the edition and chapter placement. The Cambridge material is a publisher-hosted opening preview, not a complete chapter. Its text and extraction operations were frozen before acquiring the FL opening.

The nominated [FL target](https://forgottenlanguages-full.forgottenlanguages.org/2019/03/su-dackei-ni-lascdir.html) produced 174 words under the fixed 180-word complete-sentence cap through a URL-restricted indexed result. Its page open failed with a cache miss. The imported extraction is explicitly indexed evidence; it does not claim a raw or current FL HTTP response. All 174 selected token identities and Unicode spans remain in the denominator, including the two provisional content-roster surface forms near the end. No extra text was selected to obtain more lexical evidence.
